Beerify support

Something off with your tab, room, map, or live game? Email kpuchkov1@gmail.com. Include your device/browser, what you tapped, and any message shown. Never email room credentials, a home address, or sensitive health information.

Quick fixes

  • Room will not update: keep Beerify open, check the connection, then leave and rejoin with the same six-character code. Queued drink updates retry automatically.
  • Live game is paused: the original host may be offline. After 60 seconds the oldest connected player can claim control.
  • No nearby pubs: allow location after tapping the button. A denied permission can be changed in browser or iOS settings. Provider outages fall back to cached lists and external directions.
  • Motion does nothing: start Heads Up with its permission button. You can always use the on-screen buttons instead.
  • Data did not sync: local data remains the working copy. Check your sign-in email and use “Sync now” in Profile.

Data and safety

Export your local data or delete an optional cloud account from Profile. Read the full privacy and safety notice.

Do not use Beerify BAC estimates to decide whether to drive. They are not measurements and can be wrong. If someone may have alcohol poisoning or is in immediate danger, call local emergency services.

About the estimate

Beerify’s model is informed by the Watson body-water model and the Jones review of alcohol elimination rates. Individual absorption and elimination vary widely.
