Privacy, data & safety

Beerify is not a breathalyser, doctor, or driving test. BAC values are rough statistical estimates and may be wrong. Never use Beerify to decide whether to drive, take medication, or seek care. If you have been drinking, do not drive. Call local emergency services if someone is hard to wake, breathing irregularly, confused, vomiting repeatedly, or otherwise in danger.

Age and scope

Beerify is for adults who are at least 18 and also meet the legal drinking age where they are. We ask for a one-time confirmation; we do not collect your date of birth for that confirmation.

What stays on your device

Your profile, optional age/height inputs used for estimates, weight and sex inputs, preferences, participation modes, custom drinks, favourites, drink and water logs, night history, saved crawl draft, optional compressed avatar photo, ride URL, and home address are stored in this browser or app container by default. Profile photos are never included in room state. Removing the app or clearing site storage can remove this data unless you export or optionally sync it.

Rooms and live games

When you start a Group night, Beerify sends the room code, display name, room credentials, shared crawl stops, drink summaries, reactions, scores, game actions, and connection timestamps to the Beerify API hosted on Vercel. Temporary room and game state is stored in Upstash Redis and expires. Personalized game answers are returned only to the relevant authenticated player or revealed by game rules. Short operational metrics may count actions such as invite opens and first drinks; they are not advertising profiles.

Optional account sync

If Supabase is configured and you choose account sync, Supabase processes your email sign-in and the Beerify data you sync, including an optional saved crawl draft. Sync is optional and local use remains available without an account.

Notifications

In the installed iOS app, enabling room alerts registers an Apple Push Notification service (APNs) token. Beerify and Apple process that token to deliver room updates. You can turn notifications off in iOS Settings.

Maps and location

Beerify requests location only after you tap a location control. Approximate coordinates are sent to Beerify’s bounded maps proxy to find nearby pubs and walking routes. Town and postcode text is sent through the proxy to the configured geocoding provider. Hosted map tiles load as you view the map and their provider receives normal network information such as IP address and the viewed area; Beerify does not prefetch tiles. Nearby lists can be cached locally and proxy responses can be cached briefly. The default fallbacks use OpenStreetMap-compatible services. See the OpenStreetMap tile policy.

External services and links

  • Brand artwork may load through Google’s favicon service, which receives the normal network request information.
  • Opening Apple Maps, Google Maps, or a configured ride provider leaves Beerify. If you saved a home address, it is handed to that service only when you tap the link.
  • Vercel and the configured infrastructure receive ordinary technical data such as IP address, user agent, and request logs needed to serve and protect the service.

BAC model

Estimates use a Watson body-water calculation when enough profile data is available and a more general distribution factor otherwise. Elimination is shown as a range because real people vary substantially. Background reading: Watson et al. and the Jones elimination-rate review.

Your choices

You can export local data in Profile, clear local app/site storage, end a Group night, disable notifications or location permission, and delete an optional cloud account from Profile. For access or deletion questions about service-side data, email kpuchkov1@gmail.com.
